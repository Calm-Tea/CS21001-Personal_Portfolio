I enjoy solving problems that require logical thinking and finding ways to make solutions more efficient as I find it really rewarding. What do I enjoy about my studies?

My favourite part of my studies so far has been learning about the mathematics behind computing. I found logic particularly interesting. Although it wasn't the easiest subject to grasp initially, I found it especially useful when developing more complex programs, as it helped me break problems down and simplify solutions that might otherwise have been unnecessarily complicated.

I also liked our Artificial Intelligence module, where we had the opportunity to make a search-based AI and train a machine learning model using a dataset. I enjoyed being able to apply theoretical concepts to practical programming tasks.

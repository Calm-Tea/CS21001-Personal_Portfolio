My interests

Reading

I really enjoy reading. At the moment, I'm finally making my way through The Lord of the Rings, having watched the films countless times. I've always enjoyed the world Tolkien created, so it's been interesting experiencing the story in its original form. I would also like to get around to finally reading the Hobbit.

Pool

I've also recently started playing pool, which I've found to be a surprising combination of strategy, precision, and socialising. It's a great way to meet different people while also giving me something new to learn and improve at.

Few facts about me

My life in Scotland

I moved here in 2018 and was most surprised by how talkative (in a positive way) random people you meet on the street were. It's always nice to have a chat with someone and learn a bit of their story, where they're going right now, or about how the buses are often late.

My least favourite part by far is the weather. It's far too grey and dark for my liking.

My time at University

Dundee has been wonderful. At the open days, we were told Dundee is the sunniest place in Scotland and to be honest, I didn't believe it in the slightest. I was very pleasantly surprised when I moved here and everything they said was in fact true.

I'm finding the degree to be quite fascinating and really enjoyable. I was expecting not to like quite a few modules, as I had always been told that I wouldn't enjoy everything. They were right, but even the modules I don't particularly enjoy have been very informative and have taught me something new.
